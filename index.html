Reading this brief

What the numbers mean. Breach times, canal capacities, surge stages, flood extents, and rescue totals are drawn from the IPET, ILIT and ASCE investigations and related public records. Continuous depth curves between documented checkpoints are interpolations for legibility.

What the map is. Basemap imagery/streets are real (Esri / CARTO · OSM). Neighborhood outlines are the official Orleans Parish neighborhood polygons (civic open data via Click That Hood), with St. Bernard (Arabi / Chalmette) added for the MR-GO story. Outfall canals use OSM water polygons plus channel centerlines. Floodwater spreads from breach points into low ground (elevation-aware front), then fills each basin. Click any neighborhood, breach, or pump for the dossier.

The core finding, per ASCE: roughly two-thirds of the flooding would not have happened had the floodwalls held. The 17th St wall failed with water ~5 ft below its crown; London Avenue failed ~4 ft below design stage; the pumps — built for rainfall, powered from inside the bowl — never had a move to make.
